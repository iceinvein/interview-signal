Done! I've implemented `maxSlidingWindow` using a **deque-based approach** for optimal O(n) performance.

The algorithm maintains a deque of indices in decreasing order of their values:
- **Remove outdated indices** from the front when they fall outside the current window
- **Remove smaller elements** from the back to keep the deque in decreasing order
- **Add the current index** to the deque
- **Extract the maximum** (front of deque) once we have a complete window

This efficiently handles the constraints: large arrays (up to 100k elements), negative numbers, and any window size, all in linear time with O(k) extra space.
