Done. The function manages an inventory system with add/remove operations and undo/redo support:

**Key approach:**
- Maintains an inventory map and two stacks: one for recorded actions, one for undone actions
- **Add/Remove**: Only successful removes (when stock is sufficient) are recorded and clear the redo stack
- **Undo**: Reverts the last action and pushes it to the redo stack
- **Redo**: Re-applies the most recently undone action
- Returns items sorted lexicographically with quantities > 0
