Done! I've implemented the minimum window substring algorithm using a sliding window approach:

**Algorithm:**
1. Build a frequency map of characters needed from `t`
2. Use two pointers to maintain a sliding window
3. Expand the window by moving `right` until it contains all required characters with their frequencies
4. Once valid, contract from `left` while keeping the window valid, tracking the minimum
5. When a character's frequency drops below its requirement, the window becomes invalid again

**Key details:**
- `formed` tracks how many unique characters have reached their target frequency
- When `formed === required`, we have a valid window
- We contract the window greedily from the left to minimize its length
- Time: O(|s| + |t|), Space: O(1) since ASCII letters are bounded
