**What's there**
- **Logic:** `src/orderController.js` holds all the rules.
  - The PENDING queue is always sorted VIP first, then by order number. That one rule gives "VIP behind existing VIPs, ahead of Normal", and it also puts an order back in its original spot when a bot is removed.
  - `- Bot` removes the newest bot, stops its timer and returns its order to PENDING.
  - `+ Bot` picks up pending orders immediately, and idle bots pick up new orders immediately.
- **CLI:** `src/cli.js` is an interactive prompt (`npm start`). Its commands are `normal`, `vip`, `+bot`, `-bot`, `status`, `help` and `exit`. Every output line carries an `HH:MM:SS` timestamp.
- **Scripts:** `scripts/test.sh`, `build.sh` and `run.sh` are filled in. `run.sh` writes `scripts/result.txt`, which is the path the workflow checks.
- **Tests:** they use fake timers, so they don't wait 10 seconds per order. They cover priority ordering, unique increasing order numbers, the 10-second processing, idle bots, and bot removal with the order returning to its place.
- **Docs:** I added a short "Solution" section to `README.md`.

**Things to know**
