Done. I've implemented the rain water trapping algorithm using a two-pointer approach:

**How it works:**
- Two pointers start at opposite ends, tracking the maximum height seen from each side
- At each step, we move the pointer with the smaller height inward
- Water trapped at a position equals the minimum of the two max heights minus the current height
- Time: O(n), Space: O(1)

**Example:** For `[0,1,0,2,1,0,1,3,2,1,2,1]`, it correctly calculates 6 units of trapped water.
