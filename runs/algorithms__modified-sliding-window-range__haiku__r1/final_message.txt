Done! I've implemented `slidingWindowRange` using a **monotonic deque approach** for O(n) efficiency.

**How it works:**
- Two deques track **indices** of potential maximum and minimum values in the current window
- **maxDeque** maintains indices in decreasing order of their values (max at front)
- **minDeque** maintains indices in increasing order of their values (min at front)
- For each element, remove outdated indices and prune smaller/larger candidates
- Once the window is full, the front of each deque gives the max/min of that window

**Time complexity:** O(n) — each element is added/removed from deques at most once
**Space complexity:** O(k) — deques hold at most k indices
