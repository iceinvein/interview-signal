**How it behaves:**
- **Queue order:** Pending orders are kept sorted with VIP first, then by order number. That gives the required "VIP behind existing VIPs, ahead of normals". It also means an order handed back by a removed bot goes straight back to its original position.
- **Bots:** `+bot` and every new order immediately hand work to idle bots, and a bot with nothing to do goes IDLE. `-bot` removes the newest bot, cancels its 10-second timer and puts its order back in PENDING.
- **Order numbers:** They are unique and increasing.

**CLI:** `npm start` gives the interactive prompt with `normal`, `vip`, `+bot`, `-bot`, `status`, `help` and `quit`. `npm run demo` (what `run.sh` uses) plays a scripted scenario in real time, about 35 seconds. It covers VIP priority, a bot removed mid-order, and bots finishing and going idle.

**Files:**
- `src/orderController.js`: the core logic.
- `src/cli.js`: the interactive prompt and the `--demo` mode.
- `test/orderController.test.js`: the unit tests, which use mocked timers so the 10-second waits run instantly.
- `scripts/test.sh`, `scripts/build.sh` and `scripts/run.sh`: the scripts the workflow calls. The repo's folder is `scripts/`, not `script/` as the brief says, so I followed the workflow. `build.sh` only syntax-checks the source, because there's no compile step.
