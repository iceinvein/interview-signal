**What's in it**
- **Core logic:** `src/orderController.js` holds the queue and bot rules with no I/O.
- **Priority:** VIP orders queue behind existing VIP orders and ahead of normal ones. Order numbers only go up.
- **Bots:** A new bot picks up the first pending order straight away. It finishes it after 10 seconds, then takes the next one, or goes IDLE if none are left. `-bot` removes the newest bot, stops its timer, and puts its order back in its original place in the queue.
- **Interactive CLI:** Run `node src/index.js` and use `normal`, `vip`, `+bot`, `-bot`, `status`, `help` and `quit`. Bot events print with `HH:MM:SS` timestamps. Add `--processing-ms 1000` to shorten the cooking time when trying it by hand.
- **Demo run:** `./scripts/run.sh` plays a scripted scenario of about 45 seconds and writes `scripts/result.txt`. The scenario covers VIP priority, a bot removed mid-order, and an idle bot picking up a new order. The output has the timestamps the workflow greps for.
- **Docs:** I added a "Solution" section to `README.md` covering the layout, commands and design choices.
