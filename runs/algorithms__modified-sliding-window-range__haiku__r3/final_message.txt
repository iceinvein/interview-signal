Done! I've implemented `slidingWindowRange` using a **monotonic deque** approach:

**Algorithm:**
- Maintain two deques to track indices of potential max and min values
- For each new element, remove out-of-window indices from the front
- Keep the deques monotonic by removing "useless" elements from the back (elements that won't be the extremum in any future window)
- Once a full window exists, the front of each deque gives the max/min, so compute their difference

**Complexity:**
- **Time:** O(n) — each element is processed and removed at most once
- **Space:** O(k) — deques hold at most k elements

This efficiently handles the constraints (up to 100,000 elements) without needing to rescan each window.
