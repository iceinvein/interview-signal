**What's in it:**
- `src/orderController.js` holds the order and bot logic, with no I/O.
- `src/cli.js` is the interactive CLI and the `--demo` mode. Interactive commands are `normal`, `vip`, `+bot`, `-bot`, `status`, `help` and `quit`. Every event line carries an `HH:MM:SS` timestamp.
- `README.md` documents usage, design and assumptions, with the original assignment kept below it.
- The pending list is always kept in order: VIP first, then Normal, each by order number. That gives the VIP queueing rule, and an order interrupted by `-bot` goes back into its original slot.
- Removing a bot cancels its timer, so a destroyed bot never completes an order. If another bot is idle, it picks the order up straight away.

**Assumption to check:** "original position" means the same slot by priority and order number. An interrupted order stays ahead of newer orders of its type.
