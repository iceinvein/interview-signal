Done! I've implemented the rain water trapping function with the following approach:

**Algorithm:**
1. **Build leftMax array**: For each position `i`, store the maximum height from the left (including `leftWall`)
2. **Build rightMax array**: For each position `i`, store the maximum height from the right (including `rightWall`)
3. **Calculate water**: For each bar, the water level is `min(leftMax[i], rightMax[i])`, and the water trapped is the difference between water level and bar height

**Complexity:**
- Time: O(n) — three linear passes
- Space: O(n) — for the two auxiliary arrays

The solution correctly handles:
- Empty heights array (returns 0)
- Walls of any height (including 0 for open sides)
- The constraint that water level at each position is determined by the lower of the two sides
