Done! I've implemented `maxSlidingWindow` using a deque-based approach.

**Algorithm:**
- Maintain a deque of indices in decreasing order of their values
- For each position, remove old indices outside the window and smaller elements from the back
- The front of the deque always contains the index of the maximum in the current window
- Time complexity: O(n), Space complexity: O(k)

The solution efficiently handles the constraints by processing each element exactly once.
