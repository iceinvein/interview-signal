I wrote `shortestTeleportPath` in `solution.ts`, but I haven't run it or tested it, including on your `"Sa#a.E"` example.

It's a breadth-first search over cells. A move is a step to an open neighbour, or a jump to the twin if you're standing on a portal. Each cell is visited at most once, so it runs in O(rows × columns) and handles the 1000×1000 limit. It returns the distance when it reaches `E`, or `-1` if the search runs out of cells first. It has no imports.
