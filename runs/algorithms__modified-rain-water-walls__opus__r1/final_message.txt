I've implemented `rainWaterWithWalls` in `solution.ts`, and it matches a brute-force version of your formula on 20,000 random inputs.

**How it works:** it moves two pointers inward from both ends. The left running maximum starts at `leftWall` and the right one starts at `rightWall`. At each step it fills whichever side has the lower maximum. That's safe because the other side is known to have something at least that tall, so the lower maximum is the water level for that bar. It takes O(n) time and O(1) extra space, and needs no imports.

**Checks:**
- An empty array returns `0`.
- The standard example `[0,1,0,2,1,0,1,3,2,1,2,1]` with both walls at `0` returns `6`.
- The largest case (100,000 zero-height bars between two walls of height 100,000) returns `10000000000`. That's above the 32-bit integer limit but well within the range JavaScript numbers store exactly.
